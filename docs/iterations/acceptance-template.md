# Human acceptance for each round

For each candidate, identify the decision the student needs to make and the consequence of getting it wrong. Choose a representative task from the behaviour changed in that round. Include another scenario only when a distinct material risk remains.

| What the reviewer needs | What to provide |
|---|---|
| Candidate and technical evidence | Identify the revision and link the actual result. Carry forward checks the student has already confirmed. The developer or CI runs prerequisite, boundary and regression checks. |
| A business decision | Describe the actor, situation and exact action. Provide a short output excerpt for a backend round or a direct task in the running application for a UI round. |
| A way to decide | Explain the expected business outcome, the observable sign of failure and any material limit of the evidence. Offer a focused reproduction if an observation differs. |
| A record of ownership | Record the student's actual observation or evidence reviewed, reason and decision to accept, request a change or seek clarification. Record authorisation for the next round separately. |

During a guided review present one decision at a time. A round can use Chinese or bilingual acceptance notes. Keep the student's judgement pending until they provide it.
